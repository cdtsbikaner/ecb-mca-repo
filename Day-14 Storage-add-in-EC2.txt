===============================================================
1) EBS Volume 
==========

 - Provides raw block-level storage volumes for EC2 instances
 - Works like a virtual hard drive

Ideal for:
 - Databases (MySQL, PostgreSQL)
 - Operating systems
 - Applications requiring low latency

Types:
 
 - SSD (fast performance – gp3, io1/io2)
 - HDD (throughput optimized – st1, sc1)
