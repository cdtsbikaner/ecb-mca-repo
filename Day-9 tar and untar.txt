Backup in linux 
	tar  ->  Backup  			( *.tar)
	tar  -> Shrink/Compressed  	(*.tar.gz)

 c  create
 p  preserve the bits 
 f  file
 z  zip/ shrink 
 
du -hs  /etc/

tar cpf   backup-etc-1652026.tar  /etc/

ls -ltr 

du -hs   backup-etc-1652026.tar
 
Now shrink data with tar.gz  extension: 
========================================== 
tar  cpfz  backup-shrink-etc-1652026.tar.gz --exclude=yum*  /etc/

du -hs *

How to extract any tar  /  tar.gz 

	tar xfh  Filename.tar
			OR
	tar xfh  Filename.tar.gz
	
			OR
	tar xfh  Filename.tar.gz  -C  /opt/
	
	ls -ltr /opt/
	
	du -hs  /opt/
	
	ls -ltr /opt/etc/   |   grep  yum
